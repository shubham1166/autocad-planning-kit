# GPDO 2015 Schedule 2 Part 1 — key householder limits (England)

Summary for quick checking. **Not a substitute for the legislation.** Source: The Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015, as amended, and the MHCLG "Permitted development rights for householders: technical guidance". Verify before relying on it and update this file when the law changes.

## Class A — enlargement, improvement or alteration

Not permitted if:

| Ref | Limit |
|---|---|
| A.1(b) | Total ground area covered by buildings in the curtilage (other than the original house) would exceed **50%** of the curtilage area (excluding the original house footprint) |
| A.1(c) | Height of the enlarged part exceeds the highest part of the existing roof |
| A.1(d) | Eaves height of the enlarged part exceeds the eaves of the existing house |
| A.1(e) | Extends beyond a wall forming the principal elevation, or a side elevation that fronts a highway and forms a side elevation of the original house |
| A.1(f) | Single storey: extends beyond the rear wall of the original house by more than **4 m** (detached) or **3 m** (any other house), or exceeds **4 m** in height |
| A.1(g) | Larger home extension (prior approval under A.4): single storey, up to **8 m** (detached) or **6 m** (other), max **4 m** high |
| A.1(h) | More than one storey: extends beyond the rear wall of the original house by more than **3 m**, or is within **7 m** of any boundary opposite the rear wall |
| A.1(i) | Within **2 m** of the curtilage boundary and eaves height exceeds **3 m** |
| A.1(j) | Extends beyond a side wall of the original house and exceeds **4 m** height, has more than one storey, or is wider than **half the width** of the original house |
| A.1(k) | Includes a veranda, balcony or raised platform (over **0.3 m** high), a microwave antenna, chimney/flue/soil and vent pipe, or alterations to the roof |

Article 2(3) land (A.2): no cladding of the exterior; no extension beyond a side wall; no rear extension of more than one storey.

Conditions (A.3): materials of similar appearance to the existing house; upper-floor windows in a side elevation obscure-glazed and non-opening unless the opening part is more than **1.7 m** above the floor of the room; roof pitch of a two-storey extension the same as the original house so far as practicable.

## Class AA — enlargement by construction of additional storeys

Prior approval needed. House built between 1 July 1948 and 28 October 2018. Detached: up to two storeys added (one if currently single storey); others: one storey. Max height of the extended building **18 m**; each new storey's floor-to-ceiling height no more than the existing storeys' and no more than **3 m**; extended building no more than **3.5 m** (single-storey base) or **7 m** higher than the highest part of the existing roof. Not on article 2(3) land. Verify the full conditions in the legislation.

## Class B — additions to the roof (dormers)

Not permitted if:

| Ref | Limit |
|---|---|
| B.1(b) | Higher than the highest part of the existing roof |
| B.1(c) | Extends beyond the plane of an existing roof slope forming the principal elevation and fronting a highway |
| B.1(d) | Additional roof space volume exceeds **40 m³** (terraced) or **50 m³** (any other house) — includes previous roof additions |
| B.1(e) | Includes a veranda, balcony or raised platform, or a chimney/flue/soil and vent pipe |
| B.1(f) | On article 2(3) land |

Conditions (B.2): materials similar in appearance; edge of the enlargement closest to the eaves set back at least **0.2 m** from the eaves so far as practicable (hip-to-gable excepted); side-elevation windows obscure-glazed and non-opening below **1.7 m** above floor.

## Class C — other alterations to the roof (rooflights etc.)

Not permitted if it protrudes more than **0.15 m** beyond the plane of the roof slope, is higher than the highest part of the roof, or includes chimney/flue/soil pipe/solar equipment (those are other classes). Side-elevation windows: obscure-glazed and non-opening below **1.7 m**.

## Class D — porches

Not permitted if the external ground floor area exceeds **3 m²**, any part is more than **3 m** above ground level, or any part is within **2 m** of a boundary with a highway.

## Class E — outbuildings

Not permitted if:

| Ref | Limit |
|---|---|
| E.1(b) | Total ground area covered by buildings (other than the original house) exceeds **50%** of the curtilage (excluding original house) |
| E.1(c) | Situated on land forward of a wall forming the principal elevation |
| E.1(d) | More than a single storey |
| E.1(e) | Height exceeds **4 m** (dual-pitched roof), **2.5 m** if within **2 m** of the boundary, or **3 m** in any other case |
| E.1(f) | Eaves height exceeds **2.5 m** |
| E.1(g) | Within the curtilage of a listed building |
| E.1(h) | Includes a veranda, balcony or raised platform (over **0.3 m**) |
| E.1(i) | Is a dwelling or a microwave antenna |
| E.1(j) | Container with capacity over **3,500 litres** |

Article 2(3) land: no outbuilding between a side wall and the boundary; in National Parks, National Landscapes, the Broads and World Heritage Sites, no outbuilding more than **20 m** from any wall of the house with area over **10 m²**.

## Class F — hard surfaces

Hard surface between the principal elevation and a highway over **5 m²** must be porous/permeable, or drain to a permeable area within the curtilage.
